Made-to-order paddle orifice plates and restriction plates

O-Plates is a self-service path for buyers who already know the required plate geometry. Configure the plate outside diameter, bore, handle dimensions, thickness, tolerance, marking, quantity, and lead time online.

Materials and thicknesses

The current configurator supports 304 stainless steel, 316 stainless steel, and carbon steel in enabled nominal thicknesses from 0.125 to 0.500 inch.

Dimensional configuration

Configure plate outside diameters up to 48 inches and bores up to 19 inches, along with handle width, handle length from bore center, bore tolerance, optional chamfer, and optional handle marking.

Replacement and restriction service

Use known dimensions from an existing plate, print, or approved specification to configure a replacement or restriction plate. O-Plates does not substitute for application engineering.

Buyer questions

Orifice plate ordering FAQ

Can I buy an orifice plate online?

Yes. Configure a supported plate, review its price, and continue to secure checkout. Multi-plate carts and PDF quotes are available for project purchasing.

What materials are currently available?

The active product configuration supports 304 stainless steel, 316 stainless steel, and carbon steel. The quote application remains the authority for current material and thickness availability.

Can O-Plates make a replacement plate?

Yes, when you have the known dimensions and requirements needed by the configurator. Enter the existing plate or approved-print dimensions and review the generated configuration drawing.

What documentation is available?

The ordering workflow produces a PDF quote and a configuration drawing. If your order requires material certification or inspection documentation, contact O-Plates to confirm the required documentation package before ordering.

How quickly can a plate ship?

Available estimated ship dates are shown while you configure the plate and are reflected in the quote. Timing remains subject to material availability and order review.

What if I need help selecting the bore or specification?

Contact O-Plates before ordering. The self-service configurator prices known requirements; it is not an application-engineering or flow-sizing service.
